Game plan

Ten dishes want the oven. Here is the order that works.

Times assume dinner at 4:00 pm with one standard oven. Shift everything together if you eat earlier or later.

  • Tuesday, Nov 24
  • EveningShop. Salt the chicken and leave it uncovered in the fridge. Cube the stuffing bread and leave it out to dry.
  • Wednesday, Nov 25
  • MorningBake both pies (pumpkin first at 425°F, then sweet potato at 350°F). Cool, cover, refrigerate.
  • MiddayBake regular cornbread, a double batch: one pan for the table, one for the dressing. Bake Mexican cornbread.
  • AfternoonMake pasta salad. Cook the rice. Chop all onion, celery, garlic and jalapeño.
  • EveningAssemble broccoli cheese rice, green bean casserole, dressing and stuffing in their dishes, unbaked. Cover and refrigerate. Keep fried onions and cracker topping off until baking.
  • Thursday, Nov 26
  • 12:45 pmChicken out of the fridge. Rub with spice butter. Oven to 425°F. Take the four casseroles out to lose their chill.
  • 1:30 pmChicken into the oven, middle rack.
  • 2:20 pmSweet potatoes onto the lower rack.
  • 2:50 pmChicken out when the thigh reads 165°F. Tent with foil and rest. Sweet potatoes out. Oven down to 350°F.
  • 2:55 pmDressing, stuffing, broccoli cheese rice and green bean casserole go in, two per rack.
  • 3:15 pmStovetop: mac and cheese, then honey carrots, then sweet corn.
  • 3:40 pmCasseroles out. Cornbreads in for 8 minutes to warm. Make pan gravy. Carve the chicken.
  • 4:00 pmEat. Pies come out of the fridge now so they are cool, not cold, for dessert.
If the day gets tight

Three easy trims

  • Dressing and stuffing are close cousins. Make the one you love and skip the other.
  • Buy one of the two pies. See the Order tab for bakeries.
  • Mac and cheese, carrots and corn all cook on the stove in under 20 minutes each, so they are the safest to leave for last.

Before you order anything

Ask about pork and beef

Southern sides often hide pork: green beans cooked with ham, dressing with sausage, collards with bacon. Several meals below include ham or brisket by default. Ask the kitchen and swap those items.
